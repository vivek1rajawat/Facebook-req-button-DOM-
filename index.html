Yeh sirf tumhare liye hai…

Ek baat kehni thi... lekin har kisi se nahi. Agar tum samajhna chahti ho, toh andar aana.

Shayad yeh tum nahi ho...

Jab sab kuch kehne ke liye lafz kam pad jaayein…

Ho sakta hai baatein ruki ho, par meri feelings nahi...

Main na kabhi tumhe bhool paaya, na bhoolna chahta hoon.

Har din, har pal... tum meri yaadon mein ho — bina kisi shikayat ke.

Shayad tum laut ke na aao. Shayad tum mujhe maaf bhi na karo.

Par tumhare naam likha yeh paigam hamesha yahin rahega…

Sirf tumhare liye.

Main wahi hoon – jahan tum mujhe chhod gayi thi, bina badle... bas intezaar mein.

Main jaanta hoon sab kuch wapas pehle jaisa nahi ho sakta...

Lekin yeh paigam, yeh jazbaat — hamesha waise hi rahenge, jaise tumhare saath the.

Tum kabhi wapas aayi ya nahi... yeh website, yeh sach... yeh yahan tumhara intezaar karta rahega.
